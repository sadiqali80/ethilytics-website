Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Job posted: August 11, 2026

About Ethilytics

Ethilytics is a new, UK-based startup building AI-driven solutions for the modern workplace, technology that teams can actually trust. We're a small, founder-led team moving fast, thinking long-term, and building a growing suite of solutions around one principle: intelligence with integrity. EPIP is the first of several AI-powered solutions already taking shape on our roadmap. Join us early, and help set the standard the rest of Ethilytics is built on.

The Role

As Ethilytics' founding Chief Technology Officer, you will own our technology function end to end, strategy, architecture, security and delivery, and report directly to the CEO as a true technical partner in shaping where the company goes next. This is a hands-on leadership role: you'll set the technical direction for every solution on our roadmap, build and lead a multidisciplinary team of engineers, data scientists and AI/ML specialists, and have the final word on the technical decisions that define how Ethilytics builds. You'll join early enough to shape the architecture, the team and the standards the rest of the company is built on.

What You Will Do

  • Define and own Ethilytics' technology strategy, architecture and roadmap across our full portfolio of solutions, not a single product.
  • Build, lead and grow a multidisciplinary team of software engineers, data scientists, and AI/machine learning programmers and developers as the company scales past pilot.
  • Bring a digital innovation mindset: scout emerging AI and technology trends, and turn them into new solutions, features and a genuine competitive edge for Ethilytics.
  • Own the integration layer connecting our solutions to the tools teams already use, with privacy and security engineered in from day one, not added on afterwards.
  • Work directly with compliance and legal to keep every product aligned with UK GDPR and on a credible path to ISO/IEC 27001 and 27701.
  • Own security posture end to end: architecture reviews, incident response, and honest answers when a customer's DPO asks hard questions.
  • Represent the technology function to the board and to customers, translating AI and engineering decisions into commercial outcomes.
  • Make the build-versus-buy calls a pre-launch, multi-solution company has to make, and be accountable for them.

Eligibility

  • BSc in Computer Science / Engineering, or MSc in Computer Science / Engineering (or equivalent practical experience).
  • Practical, hands-on experience and knowledge of AI and Machine Learning, applied to real digital products, not just theory.
  • A minimum of 10 years' experience in a CTO or equivalent senior technology leadership role.
  • Proven experience building and scaling digital products from concept to production.
  • Demonstrated ability to lead a multidisciplinary team spanning software engineers, data scientists, and AI/machine learning programmers and developers.

Location

Remote

Compensation & Employment Type

Permanent, Full-Time.

Competitive compensation based on experience, exposure and skills.
