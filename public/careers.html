Chief Technology Officer

Remote-first (UK) · Full-time · Founding technical hire

EPIP gives organisations team-level productivity insight without watching individuals: no screenshots, no keystrokes, no personal scores. That only works if the engineering underneath it is right. We're looking for a CTO to own that engineering, from the read-only integrations that bring data in, through the anonymisation and aggregation pipeline that makes individual re-identification impossible, to the infrastructure a DPO can sign off without a fight.

What you'll do

  • Own the technical architecture and roadmap for EPIP, from pilot through general availability.
  • Lead the read-only integration layer that connects EPIP to the tools teams already use, with no endpoint agents and nothing capturing screens or keystrokes to secure.
  • Hold the line on the anonymisation and aggregation pipeline: k-anonymity, differential privacy, and no path back to an individual, ever.
  • Build the engineering team as the company grows past pilot, starting with the first few hires.
  • Work directly with compliance and legal to keep the platform aligned with UK GDPR and on a credible path to ISO/IEC 27001 and 27701.
  • Own security posture end to end: architecture reviews, incident response, and honest answers when a customer's DPO asks hard questions.
  • Make the build-versus-buy calls a pre-launch team has to make, and be accountable for them.

What we're looking for

  • Experience leading engineering at an early-stage company, ideally through a pilot-to-production stage.
  • A genuine grounding in data privacy and security architecture, not just familiarity with the terms.
  • Comfort working with distributed systems and data pipelines at a technical level, not only a management one.
  • Direct experience with UK GDPR in a live product, including working alongside compliance or legal stakeholders.
  • The ability to hire and lead a small engineering team, and still write code while the team is this size.

Nice to have

  • Experience with differential privacy or k-anonymity techniques in a production system.
  • Involvement in an ISO/IEC 27001 or 27701 certification process.
  • Experience integrating with workplace tools such as project trackers, ticketing systems, or version control platforms.
  • Experience in HR tech, compliance tech, or another trust-sensitive B2B category.

This is the only open role at Ethilytics right now. If it isn't quite right for you but you think you'd be a strong fit for what we're building, get in touch anyway at info@ethilytics.co.uk.
