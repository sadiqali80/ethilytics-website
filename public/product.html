How we turn workplace activity into team-level insight—without monitoring individuals.

Work happens across remote, hybrid and in-office teams. Organisations need to understand where work flows smoothly, where it slows down, and where bottlenecks emerge—without watching or monitoring individual employees.

What EPIP never does

For this product, the negatives are the point.

There is no setting to switch these on. We never collect the underlying data, so there is nothing to enable.

No screenshots

We never capture or store images of anyone's screen.

No keystroke logging

Typing, shortcuts and inputs are never recorded.

No message content

We read that a task moved, never what was said.

No location or GPS

We never track where anyone physically is.

No individual scores

No personal ratings, rankings or league tables. Ever.

No personal data

No names, emails or identifiers in anything we analyse.

If we can't see it, we can't store it, and we can't leak it. You never have to defend it.

Compliance & trust

Built for UK data protection from the start.

Two ideas do the heavy lifting. In plain English:

k-anonymity, in one sentence

Every figure we show is true for a group of at least twelve people, never one. If a number could point to a single individual, it simply isn't shown.

Differential privacy, in one sentence

We add calibrated statistical noise. The team trend stays accurate, while any single person's contribution stays deniable, even to us.

Designed to satisfy your DPO
  • No personally identifiable information processed or stored
  • Data minimisation and anonymisation by design, at ingestion
  • DPIA-ready, with audit-ready records of processing
  • Aligned to UK GDPR and current ICO guidance
  • Designed to meet ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27701
  • UK data residency available for pilot customers

Pre-launch status: these are the standards EPIP is designed and built to meet. Certifications are targeted, not yet awarded, and we will never claim otherwise.
