The impasse

You can't manage what you can't see. You can't surveil your way to trust either.

Work is now spread across remote, hybrid and in-office teams. Most tools force a choice between staying blind and watching individuals. That's a false choice. EPIP exists to remove it.

Surveillance software

Watches individuals

  • Screenshots, keystrokes and activity scores tied to named employees
  • Damages trust and invites grievances from the people it watches
  • A UK GDPR risk your DPO has to defend, case by case
EPIP

Sees the workflow, not the worker

  • Team-level signal only, anonymised the moment it's ingested
  • Shows where work stalls so you can plan, staff and fix the right things
  • Built for UK GDPR, with a lawful basis your DPO can sign off
How EPIP works

From workflow signal to team-level insight, no individual ever identified.

1

Non-invasive ingestion

EPIP reads workflow metadata from the tools your team already uses, never screens, keystrokes or messages.

2

Anonymisation at source

Identity is stripped at ingestion. No PII is ever stored, so there's nothing on any individual to re-identify.

3

Privacy-preserving aggregation

Insight is computed across groups using k-anonymity (k ≥ 12) and differential privacy, so no one can be singled out.
