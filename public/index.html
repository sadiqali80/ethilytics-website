The impasse

You can't manage what you can't see. You can't surveil your way to trust either.

Work is now spread across remote, hybrid and in-office teams. You need to know where work flows smoothly and where it stalls, so you can plan, staff, and fix the right things.

But the usual answer is monitoring software that watches individuals: screenshots, keystrokes, activity scores. It damages trust, invites grievances, and under UK GDPR it becomes a risk your DPO has to defend. So most teams choose to stay blind. That is a false choice. EPIP exists to remove it.
