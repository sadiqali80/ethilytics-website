About us

Built by people who manage hybrid teams themselves.

Ethilytics builds EPIP for UK teams navigating hybrid and remote work, where the usual answer to "how is everyone doing" is either a guess or invasive surveillance software. We think there is a better way: aggregate, privacy-first signals that tell managers what is actually going on, without watching any individual.

We are a small, UK-based team, and we design every feature to pass one test first: would we be comfortable if it were used on us?

What we believe

You can't manage what you can't see, but you can't surveil your way to trust either.

Privacy by design, not by policy

We don't collect the data we'd have to protect. Anonymisation and aggregation happen before anything is stored, not after.

Built for UK GDPR from day one

Every architecture decision starts with what a DPO would ask, not what's added afterwards to pass an audit.

Early, and honest about it

We're pre-launch, running our first pilots, and still working some things out in the open with the customers who join us.

Small enough to move fast

A small team means fewer layers between what a pilot customer needs and what ships.
