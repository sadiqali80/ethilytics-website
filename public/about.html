About us

Built by people who manage hybrid teams themselves.

Ethilytics builds EPIP for UK teams navigating hybrid and remote work, where the usual answer to "how is everyone doing" is either a guess or invasive surveillance software. We think there is a better way: aggregate, privacy-first signals that tell managers what is actually going on, without watching any individual.

We are a small, UK-based team, and we design every feature to pass one test first: would we be comfortable if it were used on us?

Vision, mission & values

What we're building toward, and the lines we won't cross to get there.

Vision

A workplace where intelligence and privacy are never a trade-off.

A future where every organisation can see how work truly happens, without ever watching the people doing it.

Mission

We build AI-driven technology solutions that turn workplace signal into trustworthy intelligence.

Starting with productivity, without ever collecting data on the individual behind it.

Our values

Privacy by design, not by promise

We don't bolt privacy onto a finished product. Anonymisation and aggregation happen before data is ever stored, so there's nothing left to misuse, leak, or defend after the fact.

Team-level, always

Every solution we build answers "how is the team doing," never "what is this person doing." That's not a policy setting, it's the only shape our data ever takes.

Intelligence that earns trust

We measure a good solution by whether a DPO, a board, and the employees it's about would all sign off on it, not by how much data it can pull in.

Built by a small team, on purpose

We stay small enough that architecture and technical decisions are still made by the people accountable for them, not diffused across layers of process.

One solution today, a principle for every solution after

EPIP is the first thing we've shipped, not the only thing we'll ever build. Every future Ethilytics solution has to clear the same bar this one does.

What we believe

You can't manage what you can't see, but you can't surveil your way to trust either.

Built for UK GDPR from day one

Every architecture decision starts with what a DPO would ask, not what's added afterwards to pass an audit.

Early, and honest about it

We're pre-launch, running our first pilots, and still working some things out in the open with the customers who join us.

Small enough to move fast

A small team means fewer layers between what a pilot customer needs and what ships.
